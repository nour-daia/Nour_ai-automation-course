# SkillBridge Lite

> A small web app that compares a person's professional profile with a selected opportunity and gives actionable AI feedback: relevant skills, potential gaps, and next steps.

---

## 🎯 Problem

### The Context

Students, recent graduates, and early-career professionals often have skills, projects, and experience, but they are scattered across CVs, portfolios, and memory. When they look at an opportunity, it is hard to see how their background connects to it.

### The Problem

It is difficult to turn scattered professional information into a clear understanding of how a profile relates to the requirements of a specific opportunity. Questions like these stay unanswered:

- Which skills in my profile relate to this opportunity?
- What evidence in my projects and experience supports my fit?
- Which requirements have I not given enough information about?
- What can I do next to improve my readiness?

### Why It Matters

Without clarity, people either apply blindly or hold back from opportunities they could grow into. A clear, structured view of strengths and gaps helps them decide what to improve and what to document better.

---

## 💡 Solution

SkillBridge Lite is a focused prototype, not a full recruitment platform. The user keeps a professional profile, explores a few ready-made opportunities, and asks for a fit analysis.

What does the system do?

1. Lets the user view and edit their professional profile.
2. Shows three ready-made opportunities and their details (description, requirements, responsibilities).
3. Takes the profile and the selected opportunity and runs an **Analyze My Fit** request.
4. Displays the feedback in three sections: **Relevant Skills**, **Potential Gaps**, and **Recommended Next Steps**, and states clearly whether the result is a real AI analysis or sample data.

---

## 👤 Users

**Primary Users:**  
Students, recent graduates, and early-career professionals. This version is designed for a single user only (no roles, no admin).

**User Need:**  
Understand how their documented skills, projects, and experience relate to a specific opportunity, and know what to improve next.

## 🤖 Role of AI

AI is one function inside the solution, not the whole product.

What does AI do in this solution?

- Compares the user's profile with the selected opportunity's requirements.
- Identifies skills and experience in the profile that relate to the opportunity.
- Points out requirements that the profile does not give enough information about, and suggests practical next steps.

### What AI Should NOT Do

- Invent skills or experience that are not in the profile.
- Treat missing information as proof that a skill is missing.
- Guarantee acceptance or predict hiring outcomes.
- Present its output as a final judgment of the user's abilities. It is a helpful estimate.

The analysis also distinguishes between a skill that is **listed** and **evidence** that supports it.

### Analysis Source

> ⚠️ **[Edit after testing]** Choose one and delete the other:
> - **Real AI Analysis:** the result is generated by an AI model.
> - **Sample Analysis:** the result is fixed sample data used to show the output format. It is **not** a real AI-generated analysis.
>
> The app must always show the user which one they are seeing.

## 🔄 Automation

What parts of the process are automated?

- Combining the saved profile and the selected opportunity into a single analysis request, so the user does not re-enter anything.
- Generating the feedback in a fixed structure of three sections.
- Labeling the result as real AI analysis or sample data.

## 🗂️ Data

### Input

**Profile Data**
```text
name, headline, about, skills, projects, experience
```

**Opportunity Data**
```text
title, company, description, requiredSkills, responsibilities
```

### Output

An analysis with three sections:

1. **Relevant Skills:** skills or experience in the profile related to the opportunity.
2. **Potential Gaps:** requirements without enough information in the profile, or that may need development.
3. **Recommended Next Steps:** practical actions to improve the profile or readiness.

There is no database. Data lives inside the app (app state or browser storage).

## 🔑 Key Design Decisions

### Decision 1

**Decision:**  
No login, database, or backend in this version.

**Why:**  
The goal is a clear, working core flow within limited time and Lovable credits. Authentication and databases would add complexity without helping the core flow.

### Decision 2

**Decision:**  
Three fixed, ready-made opportunities instead of opportunity management.

**Why:**  
It keeps the user flow focused on analysis. Managing or saving opportunities is a different feature set.

### Decision 3

**Decision:**  
The analysis is always shown in the same three sections and always labeled as real AI or sample.

**Why:**  
A fixed structure makes feedback actionable, and the label keeps the user from mistaking sample data for a real analysis.

### Decision 4

**Decision:**  
Build in stages (foundation → core experience → opportunity flow → AI analysis → review) instead of one huge prompt.

**Why:**  
Each stage can be reviewed, small fixes do not require rebuilding the app, and credits are saved.
